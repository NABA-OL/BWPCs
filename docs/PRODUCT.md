# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Colombian PC enthusiasts and gamers looking to build a custom PC or buy individual components at fair prices. Most arrive via WhatsApp or mobile. They range from beginners who need guidance to enthusiasts who know their specs — all expect a trustworthy, knowledgeable seller, not a faceless marketplace. Secondary user: the business owner (admin panel), who manages prices, components, TRM, and generates formal PDF quotes daily.

## Product Purpose

B & W is a Colombian PC assembly and component sales business. The website replaces a manual Excel → Word → PDF workflow with: (1) a public site that presents the brand and lets customers get a rough price estimate by selecting component categories, (2) a hidden admin panel for managing processor/GPU prices (in USD with TRM conversion), components (in COP), TRM settings, payment surcharges, and formal multi-option PDF quote generation. Success looks like customers arriving via WhatsApp already informed and trusting, and the owner spending zero time on manual price calculations.

## Brand Personality

Elegante, confiable, gamer — the premium local expert. Not a flashy RGB storefront, not a cheap classifieds board. The panda mascot carries approachability; the black-and-white palette carries authority. Confidence without arrogance; technical depth without alienation.

## Anti-references

- **Generic RGB gaming sites** (Thermaltake, iBUYPOWER): overcrowded, neon-heavy, every pixel screaming. The opposite of the restrained premium feel.
- **Cheap marketplace look** (MercadoLibre, Amazon listing layout): no brand identity, just product grids and prices — erodes trust.
- **Generic template sites** (Wix/Squarespace feel): obviously off-the-shelf, no real identity. B & W is a business with craft and personality.

Reference: **NZXT.com** — dark, minimal, premium gaming brand. Clean typography, confident white space, brand-first before specs. That level of restraint and craft applied to a local Colombian context.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint over flash** — every color, element, and motion earns its place. If it doesn't serve the user's decision, cut it.
2. **Trust through precision** — prices, formulas, and component specs are the product; show them clearly and honestly. No hidden complexity, no vague claims.
3. **Mobile-first, always** — most customers arrive via WhatsApp on Android. Every layout decision starts at 375px.
4. **Brand in the details** — the panda, the geometric motifs, the B&W palette applied with intent. Personality lives in the specifics, not in generic gaming clichés.
5. **Expert confidence** — speak to people who know what they want and to people learning. Never condescend, never oversimplify. The tone is the knowledgeable friend, not the pushy sales page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text, full keyboard navigation, screen reader semantics. Additionally, optimize for mobile performance: target fast load on 3G connections (minimal blocking JS, optimized images), support older Android browsers (Chrome 90+). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations.
